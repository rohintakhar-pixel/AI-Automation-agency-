# Example return policy

**This is an example. Replace every word of it with your own policy before you
switch the bot on.**

The bot only ever repeats what is written in the `returnPolicy` field of the
**Your settings** node. It does not know your policy from anywhere else, it
does not look it up on your website, and it will not invent one. If that field
is vague, the replies will be vague. If it is empty, the bot says you will
confirm the details yourself — which is honest, but not much use to a customer.

Below is the level of detail that works well. Notice that it answers the
questions a customer actually asks, in the order they ask them, and that it
says plainly what happens in the awkward cases.

---

## Example, for a small clothing shop

We accept returns within 30 days of delivery.

Items must be unworn, unwashed, and in their original packaging with the tags
still attached. We cannot accept returns on underwear, swimwear, or pierced
jewellery for hygiene reasons.

To start a return, reply to your order confirmation email with your order
number and which items you are sending back. We reply within one working day
with the return address. We do not send prepaid labels.

Return postage is paid by the customer unless the item is faulty or we sent the
wrong thing, in which case we cover it and refund your original postage too.

Once we receive the item we check it and refund within 5 working days. Refunds
go back to the card used for the order. Bank clearing usually adds another 3 to
5 days on top.

Exchanges: we do not do direct exchanges. Return the item for a refund and
place a new order, so you are not waiting on stock.

Sale items are returnable on the same terms as everything else.

If an item arrives damaged, send a photograph with your first email. We replace
or refund without asking you to send it back.

---

## Writing your own

A few things that make a noticeable difference to the replies:

- **State the window in days, and say what it counts from** — the order date or
  the delivery date. "30 days from delivery" is answerable; "a reasonable
  period" is not.
- **Say who pays return postage**, including the faulty-item case.
- **Say how long a refund takes**, and mention bank clearing separately so
  customers do not chase you on day four.
- **List anything you cannot take back**, so the bot can say no politely
  instead of leaving it for you.
- **Say what the customer does first.** Reply to this email? Fill in a form?
  The bot will tell them exactly what you write here.
- **Leave out anything you are not sure about.** If your policy does not cover
  a case, the bot says you will confirm it yourself, and it lands in your
  drafts. That is the correct outcome for an edge case.
