KITSET — SHOPIFY SUPPORT-EMAIL DRAFTER
======================================

Thank you. This is yours now. There is no licence check, nothing expires, and
the bot never contacts Kitset.


WHAT IS IN THIS DOWNLOAD
------------------------

shopify-support-drafter.workflow.json
    This is the bot. It is an n8n workflow with 16 nodes. You import this file
    into n8n. Do not open it in a text editor and edit it by hand; everything
    you need to change is a field inside n8n once it is imported.

example-return-policy.md
    An example return policy, so you can see the level of detail the bot works
    best with. Replace every word of it with your own policy. The bot only ever
    repeats what you write.

READ-ME-FIRST.txt
    This file.

The setup manual is on the Kitset website, on this bot's page. It is the same
manual you could read before buying. Keep that page open while you set up.


WHAT ORDER TO DO THINGS IN
--------------------------

1. Read the setup manual all the way through once, before doing any of it.
   It takes ten minutes and it will save you an hour.

2. Write your return policy out in plain words. Have it ready to paste.

3. Work through the manual's steps in order, 1 to 13. Do not skip ahead to
   importing the workflow; the credentials have to exist first or the import
   just shows you a canvas full of red warnings.

4. Test it by hand on one real order before you switch it on.


THE ONE THING TO KNOW BEFORE YOU START
--------------------------------------

The bot ships with automatic sending switched OFF, and the workflow ships
switched off too.

Everything it writes goes into your Gmail drafts for you to read and send. That
is deliberate. A draft you glance at costs you ten seconds. A wrong reply sent
to a customer costs you the customer.

When you have watched it for a week and the drafts have all been right, the end
of step 13 in the manual explains how to let it send the plainest replies on
its own. Even then it only ever auto-sends a straightforward "where is my
order" answer about a shipped order with a real tracking number on it. Returns
and address changes always wait for you.


WHAT IT COSTS YOU TO RUN
------------------------

Nothing further to Kitset. You paid once.

You will pay two other companies directly:

    OpenAI    typically well under $1 per month at a solo store's volume
    n8n       $0 if you self-host, about $20 per month on n8n Cloud

Shopify and Gmail cost nothing extra; you already have them.


WHAT IT DOES NOT DO
-------------------

- It reads your Shopify orders. It cannot change, refund, or cancel them, and
  it does not have permission to try.
- It answers three kinds of question: where is my order, basic return requests,
  and address changes. Anything else it leaves for you to read.
- It cannot create a return label or start a return in Shopify.
- It does not learn from your edits.
