Questions

The things people ask first.

What actually counts as "one automation"?
One repetitive process, handled end to end. For example: "every invoice that lands in accounts@ gets read, entered into our accounting tool, and flagged if it doesn't match a purchase order" is a single automation — however many steps that takes behind the scenes.
Do I have to change the tools my team already uses?
No. We build around your existing stack rather than asking you to move onto ours. If a tool has an inbox, an export, or an API, we can usually work with it. If it genuinely can't be connected, we'll tell you that on the first call instead of selling you a workaround.
What happens when the AI gets something wrong?
It escalates instead of guessing. Every automation has a confidence threshold you control, and anything below it goes to a person with the context attached. Every action is logged, so you can always see what ran, when, and on what input.
Will my company's data be used to train models?
No. Your data is used to run your automation and nothing else — we won't use it to train models or share it with anyone outside the work you've hired us for. Once we're live, this commitment goes into the contract in plain language.
What does joining the waitlist actually commit me to?
Nothing. It's an email address on a list. You'll hear from us when build slots open and when pricing is set, and you can ask to be removed at any point by replying to any message we send.
When does the first cohort start?
We're onboarding as builds free up rather than against a fixed launch date — and we won't invent one to create urgency. The waitlist is how you hear the moment there's a slot.
